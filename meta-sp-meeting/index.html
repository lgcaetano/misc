The one-paragraph version. Zoppy runs WhatsApp for about 1,300 Brazilian stores through one tech-provider app and, since July, an AI sales agent that answers customers with product catalogs as WhatsApp Flows. The four things that cost us most: no way to test the Cloud API from Brazil without a production business account; messages accepted with HTTP 200 that are then lost with no error, so a BSP cannot fall back; one opaque app-level API quota shared by all tenants, sized by a "daily active users" formula that does not describe a BSP; and platform changes that reach us through vendor blogs and forum threads instead of Meta.

A1. Onboarding through Embedded Signup and Coexistence

How we do it today (code on master, 26 Oct).
  1. Three entry points, one backend endpoint: the CRM onboarding wizard, the "Acesso restrito" card in the CRM chat, and the PLG app's "Conectar WhatsApp". All open the Facebook JS SDK popup (no redirects) with config_id, response_type: code, feature: whatsapp_embedded_signup, featureType: whatsapp_business_app_onboarding, sessionInfoVersion: 3. There is no cloud-only variant: every entry asks for the Business-app onboarding and Meta's popup decides; we infer coexistence from which FINISH event comes back.
  2. The popup's WA_EMBEDDED_SIGNUP message gives us waba_id and phone_number_id; ERROR and CANCEL events (with session_id, current_step, error_code) are logged through a dictionary of about 100 Meta signup error codes translated to PT-BR. Nothing is persisted; only logs.
  3. Backend exchanges the code for a token server-side (since May 2026; before that the app secret shipped in the JS bundle). Only access_token is kept, stored in plain text; expires_in is discarded and debug_token is never called. An expired code (30-second window) surfaces as a 422 and the user redoes the popup.
  4. We read the WABA (name, timezone, currency) and the phone (verified_name, code_verification_status, quality_rating), upsert one account row per company (appId = our tech-provider app, scenario tech_provider or tech_provider_wpp_business, active: false), one phone row, and answer "integrated with success" before anything happened at Meta.
  5. Async activation, resumable by step: POST /{waba}/subscribed_apps with an empty body (we never use override_callback_uri); read platform_type and is_on_biz_app; coexistence: if the number is on the Business app, nothing else, otherwise we silently downgrade to cloud and register; cloud: POST /{phone}/register with a hard-coded PIN 123456 (no UI ever collects the merchant's two-step PIN; 133005/133006 only get "retry"). Then origin becomes TECH_PROVIDER or WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_APP and the account is active. Ten attempts, exponential backoff, then a "failed, attempt N/10" card with a retry button.
  6. Post-activation, best effort: template sync (everything missing on the new WABA becomes PENDING_DELETION and is re-submitted under a new name, no pacing), health, messaging limit, currency. The smb_app_data state sync is no longer requested (removed 4 Sep) and inbound smb_app_state_sync is acknowledged and dropped; history sync has no caller.
  7. Webhooks: Meta → API Gateway in zoppy-event-bridge (HMAC check with our app secret) → SNS/SQS → zoppy-api. account_update events (partner removed, offboarded, violation, restriction, ban) become account alerts; PARTNER_ADDED is log-only. A 15-minute-cached status endpoint refreshes verification, review, currency, quality and messaging limit.
  8. Re-onboarding reuses the same row (WABA id swapped in place, token replaced, origin wiped until the next activation); the company loses Chat until activation completes; a WABA or phone id held by another company's row, including idle disconnected rows, blocks with "WABA_ID já registrado". Disconnect flips the row to idle and calls nothing at Meta: no DELETE subscribed_apps, no deregister, so Meta keeps sending us webhooks for it.
  9. Legacy path: 42 active accounts still use the merchant's own Meta app (support typed credentials, webhook pointed at a per-account URL). Their webhooks are signed by their app's secret, and WhatsApp Commerce Flows skip them.

We never read ownership_type, on_behalf_of_business_info or country.

Coexistence, once active.

  • 374 active accounts are known coexistence (30%); about 300 to 320 companies produce echoes every weekday (data lake, 30 days), so a good part of the 575 older accounts with no origin recorded are coexistence too. Since May, coexistence onboardings outnumber cloud-only about 2 to 1. 21 coexistence companies run our AI agent.
  • Echoes (smb_message_echoes, the merchant replying from the WhatsApp Business app) are 49% of all our inbound traffic: 36,004 echoes vs 37,269 customer messages in one weekday, 33,000 to 40,000 every weekday over the last 30 days. We store them as outbound messages (status sent, origin business_initiated, no manager, no AI trigger) and push them to the inbox. An echo never assigns a seller, never closes a room and does not pause the AI, so the agent keeps answering after the human replied from the app; worse, the AI's context extractor shows the human's app reply to the model as if it were a campaign. The Python agent has no notion of echo at all.
  • History (history phases): the strategy shipped in Jan 2026, nothing ever requests the sync from Meta and the gate column is NULL on every account, so no history message has ever been imported. Phone-book sync (smb_app_state_sync): dropped at two layers since 4 Sep; Meta still pushes it, about 600 events a day in night bursts, with payloads that risk the 256 KB SQS limit; its timestamps are in milliseconds while every other webhook is in seconds.
  • We read is_on_biz_app only during the first activation and only downgrade (coexistence → cloud); a number that joins the Business app later is never re-detected. Templates, campaigns, groups, Flows and MM Lite are not gated by coexistence.

Where clients got stuck

  • No test path. Onboarding plus first send cannot be exercised without a real customer; Meta's free test number cannot message Brazilian phones (section B1).
  • Adding Zoppy as partner reset a verified WABA's review to "not started" before any API call (Khor / Taça e Prosa, May 2026). Undocumented. The merchant's Meta ticket got an automatic "Meta AI Agent" reply about payment methods. Removing the WABA from the portfolio hung in the UI.
  • Re-onboarding in coexistence turned on the cross-country block for a Brazilian number messaging Brazilians (Lissen, 15 Sep 2026): 531 accepted, 0 delivered, 456+ with 130497, while health_status said AVAILABLE for WABA, business and app. The business "country" is not the number's DDI and is not readable on the WABA node.
  • Re-onboarding a number yields a new WABA with no templates. Templates are per WABA with no migration; today 12,540 templates in 956 companies are waiting to be recreated under a new name and re-approved, and every month of 2026 between 260 and 1,380 templates in 130 to 240 companies were orphaned this way (vs 6 to 48 rejected by Meta's review per month). Bendizê: 150 recreations in a burst hit (#4) Application request limit reached and blocked template operations for every other company. 85 templates in 16 companies fail recreation permanently and show as "Reprovado" with no reason (content rules, carousel media, "Variables can't be at the start or end of the template").
  • Coexistence activation pulled the merchant's whole phone book into the CRM. smb_app_state_sync is the address book; Meta re-sent it unprompted on 28 Aug and 1 Sep (Esportiva): 1,610 then 239 "customers" created from contacts who never bought, and a segmented campaign reached ~2,000 of them. Zoppy had requested the sync at activation as documented. The field cannot be unsubscribed separately.
  • A stored business token silently lost access to the WABA (ZHAYA SHOES, since 19 Aug): code 100 "Object with ID ... does not exist, cannot be loaded due to missing permissions" on every send; the integration looked green; no event fired.
  • Failures inside Meta's popup, before we get anything. Amori Joias: coexistence pairing failed with wa.md.pair Code=16; removing and re-adding the WABA in Business Manager did not help; closed as "no fix on our side". Naturalis: the popup showed a policy restriction while health_status said healthy; it resolved itself. Biodora: first attempt cancelled at step WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_APP_ONBOARDING_QR_CODE. There is no server-side way to fetch what happened to a signup session_id; we rely on the customer's screenshot.
  • The 30-second single-use authorization code is too short for Brazilian mobile networks. Biodora: Meta answered "This authorization code has expired" 36 ms after our POST reached the API. In 30 days: 108 signups started, 103 succeeded, 2 of the 5 failures were expired codes.
  • Coexistence activation burned the 24-hour smb_app_data window. Soul Consciente: 4 onboardings plus 7 manual resyncs times 10 attempts, about 110 sync calls, then (#4) Synchronization Request limit exceeded and (#135000) Synchronization request can only be made within 24 hours of onboarding; the account sat 7 days with a blank status line. We no longer request the sync at all, so history import from the Business app is never attempted.
  • Registration PIN. Embedded Signup gives us no way to receive or set the number's two-step PIN, so we register with a hard-coded one; a number that already has its own PIN fails with 133005 and there is no UI to enter it.
  • Forty-two stores still integrated through their own Meta app (a legacy hack). Flows endpoints are signed with that app's secret, so Zoppy's endpoint cannot serve them.

What we built

  • Dropped smb_app_state_sync entirely and a cleanup job for ghost customers (zoppy-api PR 11118).
  • Template recreation on re-integration, including carousel templates (82 models in 35 companies) and a change detector that avoids Meta calls when nothing Meta-visible changed.
  • Moved the code-to-token exchange out of the browser in May (it used to ship the app secret in the JS bundle).
Ask. A server-side way to fetch the outcome of a signup session_id (step reached, error code) and a documented list of current_step values. A longer or renewable authorization code. A way to receive or set the two-step PIN inside Embedded Signup, or registration without a PIN for numbers created there. Test-mode Embedded Signup yielding a WABA that can message +55. Document and fix the review reset on partner add. Expose the business country used for 130497 and make health reflect it. Template copy or migration when a number moves to a new WABA, or no re-review for identical content, and per-WABA template-management quotas. The phone-book sync opt-in and separate from history; a sync budget we can read. An account_update when a partner loses asset access or a phone drops off the Cloud API.

A2. The new account model: one number across more than one partner

What we assume today (code on master, 26 Oct).
  • One WABA ↔ one app ↔ one company. Inbound webhooks resolve the company by WABA id with a plain "find one" (no active filter, first row wins). Production holds the assumption: 0 WABAs with two accounts, 0 phone ids under two accounts. We have no code path for a WABA owned by another partner: ownership_type, on_behalf_of_business_info and anything "solution partner" appear nowhere in our repos.
  • Everything is bound to one Meta app: token exchange, webhook signature, Flow endpoint signature, Flow provisioning (skipped when the account's app is not ours). 1,204 active accounts are on our app; 42 legacy ones on the merchant's own app, all with no origin recorded.
  • We subscribe the app to the WABA with an empty body (no field list, no override_callback_uri); fields are whatever the App Dashboard says, which we cannot read with a business token.
  • On disconnect we call nothing at Meta, so from Meta's side we remain a partner on every WABA that ever connected.

What we have seen that touches this

  • The tech-provider webhook receives events for every WABA ever subscribed to the app, including ones Zoppy no longer serves: 828,109 of 1,692,812 deliveries over 3 days in Aug (48.9%) were for unknown WABAs, and answering 400 makes Meta redeliver. Sharing a number across partners multiplies this.
  • Our test WABA has two apps subscribed ("App Zoppy - MIRROR" with an override_callback_uri, "App Zoppy - Tech Provider" with its default callback). Meta fans every webhook out to both; production logs "account not found or inactive" for each one. That is the multi-partner situation in miniature, and it shows what breaks when a customer's number is shared: statuses for the other partner's wamids match nothing; the other partner's API sends are invisible unless Meta echoes them (unknown); template sync would import the other partner's templates and our recreation job could act on them; throughput, quality, tier and the 24-hour windows are per number, so the other partner's traffic consumes the same budget; the other partner can register or deregister the number and burn the smb_app_data sync quota; our AI's human-takeover gate cannot see a human replying through another partner.
  • Flow data endpoints are signed with the secret of "the app connected to the Flow", and the encryption public key is per phone number. With two partners on one number, which app signs, and whose key is on the number?
  • Templates, quality rating and messaging tier live on the WABA and the portfolio, not on the partner. One partner's campaigns set the tier and quality the other one lives with.
  • Subscription state is opaque to a tech provider: a business token cannot read which fields the app has on, and whether enabling a field applies to already-subscribed WABAs is stated nowhere.
Ask. In the new model: which app receives messages, statuses, smb_message_echoes, history and smb_app_state_sync when several apps are subscribed, with a deterministic owner for inbound; whether another partner's API sends are echoed to us and marked as such; token, register and deregister ownership; how templates and Flows are scoped; ownership_type and on_behalf_of_business_info on the WABA and phone objects so partners can detect sharing; a readable subscription state per WABA and per app; partner-scoped Flow keys and signing; a way to stop deliveries for a partner that was removed; and who sees quality, tier and template state when two partners share a number.

A3. Getting clients verified, including partner-led verification

How we do it today (code on master, 26 Oct).
  • We read business_verification_status, account_review_status and currency from the WABA only when a merchant opens the status report screen (15-minute cache); no job syncs them. That is why 677 of 1,246 active accounts have the field NULL. The account_review_update webhook writes the decision; PARTNER_CLIENT_CERTIFICATION_STATUS_UPDATE is received and discarded (log only; card open since May).
  • Display name: we read verified_name on that same screen and do not persist it; name_status is never requested and phone_number_name_update has no handler, so a display-name rejection (131037) is invisible to us.
  • The CRM shows one badge computed from both statuses: "Verificada", "Pendente" ("você pode continuar enviando normalmente") or "Reprovada" (with a verification tooltip even when the cause is a ban). Review status is never shown on its own. The PLG app explains 141010 ("A Meta limita quantas conversas a loja pode iniciar por dia", button "Verificar a empresa na Meta" → Business Manager security settings) and review rejected/pending.
  • Partner-led verification: nothing. No call to any verification endpoint, no document upload, no submit-on-behalf. Every path is a link to Business Manager. Onboarding is a single "Login com o Facebook" button; no step tells the merchant to verify the business or add a payment method before connecting. The only trace of "partner-led" in our code is Meta's own Embedded Signup error strings translated to PT-BR, including 2494143: "A empresa já iniciou ou tentou fazer a verificação com outro parceiro. No momento, não aceitamos que vários parceiros realizem a verificação."
  • CSM guidance exists only inside card comments ("complete a verificação no Gerenciador de Negócios"); the card to write a Meta-errors document and train CS has been open since May with an empty body.

Where clients got stuck

  • About 260 active accounts carry 141010 (business not verified, volume limited) and 76 carry 141006 (payment method). Zoppy shows these as "blocked" alerts although replies inside the 24-hour window keep working; the mapping from code to actual effect is not documented.
  • Five stores cannot create Flows at all: every create fails with 139000 "Blocked By Integrity"; all five also fail business verification. Not documented; "fixed by contacting Support" only.
  • business_verification_status, account_review_status and partner certification (PARTNER_CLIENT_CERTIFICATION_STATUS_UPDATE) are three different things; Zoppy confused two of them for months (card 10197) and accounts approved before a field existed stay NULL forever because the webhook only fires on change.
  • Adding Zoppy as partner reset a verified WABA's review to "not started" (A1). Zoppy has "no endpoint to revert, skip or accelerate the re-audit"; the merchant (Khor) later turned WhatsApp off and runs on e-mail only.
  • Meta refuses a second partner on verification: error 2494143 "the business already started or attempted verification with another partner; we do not currently accept multiple partners performing the verification". So a merchant who tried once with someone else is stuck.
  • Display name: a change triggered a 72-hour retry lock with no conclusion (Stucci, 16 days to resolve); display-name rejection (131037) is not visible to us at all.
  • Unik Store: an approved template failed with generic 135000; it was 141010 (unverified business, volume limited) in disguise; 5 days to find out.
Ask. One documented matrix of verification, review, partner certification, health_status (AVAILABLE / LIMITED / BLOCKED) and the 141xxx codes, with what each combination blocks and the merchant action. Partner-led verification for Tech Providers: an API or documented path to initiate, submit and track verification and payment-method setup on the merchant's behalf, with webhooks on first classification, not only on change; and lift the single-partner rule (2494143). Do not reset review on partner add, or document it with an SLA and a webhook. Document that unverified businesses cannot create Flows, and a self-serve path out of 139000. name_status in the phone webhook.

A4. Warnings before a client gets restricted, and error messages that tell you what to fix

How we do it today (code on master, 26 Oct).
  • Health is polled, not pushed. Every 6 hours (was 3; throttled to one account per 12 s so a full sweep takes about 4.2 h) we read the phone's messaging limit and quality rating and the WABA's health_status, and store one row per entity (WABA, BUSINESS, APP) with Meta's error_description and possible_solution. We never read throughput. The sweep is our single biggest consumer of the app quota; about 900 dead WABAs still cost one call each per cycle.
  • The earliest signal we ever get is the restriction itself. phone_number_quality_update arrives only on FLAGGED/UNFLAGGED (YELLOW never arrives, only polling sees it); account_update events (violation, restriction, ban, offboarded, partner removed) become account alerts after the fact; 141015 (daily business-initiated cap reached) is only ever seen in a health row up to 6 h later. We have zero account_alerts webhook rows in production, so either Meta does not send them to a tech-provider app or the field is not subscribed; we cannot tell from code. business_capability_update, business_status_update, payment_configuration_update have no handler.
  • Health → "integration issues". A health row with can_send_message false opens WPP_BUSINESS_BLOCKED / WPP_WABA_BLOCKED / WPP_APP_BLOCKED and closes on the next sweep that reads true, so recovery shows up to 6 h late. Meta's LIMITED is collapsed into "not AVAILABLE", so 141010 (unverified, volume limited) opens "Business Manager blocked" and 141006 (payment, only business-initiated blocked) opens "WABA blocked". Today: 247 open WPP_BUSINESS_BLOCKED (242 companies), 139 WPP_PAYMENT_ISSUE, 69 WPP_WABA_BLOCKED, 29 policy violation, 21 token invalid, 21 sustained delivery failure, 10 BM blocked, 4 quality red. Send-time path: a failed status with 131031 opens WPP_BLOCKED_BM, 131042 opens WPP_PAYMENT_ISSUE, closed by any later delivered message, so the payment issue flaps daily.
  • What the merchant sees. The CRM renders only 2 of the 15 issue types as banners (BM blocked, payment issue), MASTER role only, with links to Zoppy help-center articles. Business blocked, WABA blocked, quality red, token invalid, policy violation: never rendered in the CRM. The CRM status screen shows quality ("Boa/Atenção/Ruim"), the tier as "limite diário {used}/{max}" (numerator from pricing analytics, so it over-estimates), and a "Visualizar detalhes" link hard-coded to one business id and asset id for every merchant. The PLG app translates 141006/141014/141005/141010/131000/141015 with CTAs. The merchant-notification rail (e-mail/WhatsApp) has tables and a model on master but no application code.
  • Error mapping. Synchronous Meta errors are classified in @Zoppy-crm/whatsapp-v2 and given PT-BR titles in zoppy-api: token expired (reconnect), permission denied ("Facebook Developer Dashboard", useless to a merchant), rate limited (4, 17, 341, 613, 80007, 80008, 130429, 131048, 131056, 131064 all share one message), policy (368, 130497, 131031 → "Conta restrita por política... entre em contato com o suporte da Meta"), registration (133xxx, incl. 133010 → "verificação da empresa pendente"), media (131052/131053 → "verifique o formato e o tamanho", which is wrong for 131053: it is Meta's fetch proxy failing on our link), template params (132xxx), unknown → "erro inesperado (Detalhe Meta: English text)". Webhook failed statuses get PT-BR copy for 131026, 131042, 131049, 131050, 130472 only in the template test-send path. In campaigns the merchant sees "{n} falhas" and, per recipient, only the CSV export with Meta's raw English message; in the inbox, "Erro: {error_data.details}" in English. No copy at all for 130497 on webhook, 131037, 131047, 470, 135000, 139000.
  • Sustained-failure detector: 20+ failures in 15 min at 80%+ failure rate opens an issue; 131049 and 130472 are ignored because nothing can be done about them. A daily Grafana rule counts companies above failure, pending or rejection thresholds (39 companies on 26 Oct) with no per-company label. No Grafana rule watches Meta-side tenant health (quality, tier, verification, bans).

  • Template rejections are free text, often without a code. 35 rejections in 10 days: 23 were Meta rules that could be validated locally if machine-readable, 9 were "account unavailable" surfacing as "provider rejected". B3. WhatsApp Flows

Why Flows. Every non-template message is priced the same from 1 Oct, carousel is marketing-only, no interactive message holds more than one image. A 5-product catalog was 12 billable messages; one Flow is 1. So the catalog became a Flow. What it took:

Product limitations

  • No image by URL anywhere. "RichText: external URIs are not supported"; Image, NavigationList, ImageCarousel are base64 too. The only route for many photos is the encrypted data endpoint.
  • Payload limit undocumented and enforced after the 200. "1 Mb" appears only in the Image component's limits table; nothing on the send-a-Flow page. 1,178 KB → 200 then failed; 976 KB delivered in a clean chat; on one iPhone anything from 250 KB up never arrived. Whether the cap is 1,000,000 or 1,048,576 bytes and what it counts is unknown.
  • RichText renders inline images only behind data:image/png;base64,, whatever the bytes. JPEG behind image/jpeg, WebP behind image/webp, or no prefix: raw base64 printed as text. Reproduced on iPhone, Android (5 Oct) and Web. The reference lists png, jpg, WebP. Image.src has the opposite convention. Zoppy ships WebP bytes labelled png.
  • Per-screen caps: 3 Image, 2 EmbeddedLink; RichText alone on its screen except a Footer; Flow name unique per WABA; uploading an asset to a published Flow reverts it to DRAFT.
  • Lifecycle: published Flows cannot be edited (139001) or deleted (139004), only deprecated, and deprecation "prevents sending and opening", so customers tapping an old catalog get nothing. No last-sent or opened data. No documented maximum per WABA (our own bug created 48 per WABA; Meta never refused).
  • Data endpoint model is heavy for a BSP: public key per phone number (76 to 84 uploads); requests signed with "the app connected to the Flow", so 4 stores on their own app cannot be served; alerts at p90 1 / 5 / 7 s, error 5 / 10 / 50%, availability under 90%; Throttled = 10 sends per hour; Blocked = customers cannot open previously sent Flows. Throttle is per Flow but the endpoint is shared, so one slow gateway degrades every store.
  • Business verification gates Flow creation: 139000 "Blocked By Integrity" for 5 stores, support only.
  • No opened status; single-product answers can never be a Flow (one-screen terminal Flow passes validation, publishes, then send fails 131009).

Validator vs reality

  • If wrapping a Footer: validator OK, navigation fails on the client.
  • NavigationList: validator OK, "opens with an error on the iOS client in every variant tried".
  • Terminal entry screen without Footer: validator OK, publish OK, send fails 131009.
  • "WABA subscribed to Flows webhooks" publishing check: looks like a prerequisite, is a cosmetic warning (verified 17 Oct).

Docs

  • Flows changelog URL returned HTTP 500 (8 and 14 Sep); versioning, flowjson guide and FAQ URLs 404. The components reference served to a fetcher did not list ImageCarousel or NavigationList. "1 Mb" (Image table) vs "10 MB" (endpoint). Best practices say "avoid base64 images" while every component forbids URLs.
Ask. Image by URL or media id; a documented in-message limit with synchronous rejection; fix or document the RichText prefix rule; validator parity; fix NavigationList on iOS; a documented read-only catalog pattern; per-app key registration and partner-app signing; explicit Throttled/Blocked thresholds and windows; in-place update or hard delete; an opened status; document 139000 with a self-serve path.

  • The 20 mps coexistence brake never engages in production. It keys on scenario === 'tech_provider_wpp_business', but activation overwrites scenario with integrated on 100% of active accounts. Every coexistence number sends at our 30/s ceiling, above Meta's documented 20 mps for coexistence.
  • The history pipeline is dead end-to-end: nothing requests the sync, Meta never sends history, the gate column is NULL everywhere. The Jan 2026 strategy has never processed a message.
  • Echoes do not pause the AI (card 13544, open): the human's app reply is stored with no manager and shown to the model as broadcast context. 21 coexistence companies run the agent.
  • Verification status is NULL on 677 of 1,246 accounts because the only writer is the merchant opening the status screen; partner certification webhooks are received and discarded (card 7590, open since May).
  • Meta's LIMITED is collapsed into BLOCKED (card 12585): 247 merchants have an open "business blocked" issue when they are only unverified. Not rendered in the CRM anyway: 13 of 15 issue types have no banner.
  • Registration PIN is hard-coded (123456) and the Embedded Signup flow has no cloud-only variant.
  • Disconnect never tells Meta (no DELETE subscribed_apps, no deregister); idle rows keep their ids and block returning customers ("WABA_ID já registrado").
  • 12,540 templates in PENDING_DELETION across 956 companies, re-submitted without pacing under the shared app quota.
  • The CRM "Visualizar detalhes" link on the status screen is hard-coded to one business id and asset id for every merchant; the zoppy-fe verification badge compares uppercase VERIFIED while the DB holds lowercase verified (flagged, not verified in the browser).
  • We cannot read our own webhook field subscriptions (needs an app token) and have zero account_alerts and zero flows webhook rows ever; confirm in the App Dashboard which fields are on before telling Meta "we never received X".
  • The merchant-notification rail (e-mail/WhatsApp for integration issues) has tables and a model on master but no application code.
